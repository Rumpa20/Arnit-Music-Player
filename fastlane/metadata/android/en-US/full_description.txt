Arnit Music Player lets you browse and play audio stored on your device, as well as online audio streams.

Features:
- Browse local audio files and folders.
- Create playlists and manage favorites.
- Manage the playback queue and recently played tracks.
- Browse music by album and artist.
- Use media controls, an equalizer, bass boost and a sleep timer.
- Choose light or dark appearance and selectable backgrounds.
- Export and restore app data using JSON backups.
- Edit lyrics and optionally search for lyrics through LRCLIB.

Privacy:
Online lyrics search is optional and starts only when you request it. Track metadata is sent to LRCLIB for that search. Local audio files are not uploaded.
