# Arnit Music Player Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2 November 2026
**Contact:** abhijitdas021984@gmail.com

Arnit Music Player is an Android music player. This policy describes the app behaviour in version 1.7.1.

## Information stored on your device

The app can read audio media that you choose to make available through Android media permissions. App preferences, favourites, playlists, recently played information, and lyrics that you save are stored locally on your device. The app provides a backup export/import feature; backup files are saved or selected by you using Android's document picker.

## Online lyrics search

If you tap **Search online**, the app sends the song title and artist metadata to the LRCLIB service to look for lyrics. An internet connection is required. The app does not require an LRCLIB account or API key. Do not use online search if you do not want this song metadata sent to LRCLIB. Please also review LRCLIB's own privacy information and terms.

## Data sharing and advertising

The app does not include an account sign-in feature. It does not intentionally upload your local music library, playlists, favourites, or locally saved lyrics. This statement is limited to the app's current implementation and does not cover Android, your device manufacturer, your network provider, or third-party services' own handling of requests.

## Permissions

The app requests internet access for online lyrics and online playback, and audio/media access for browsing local music. Foreground media playback permissions are used to keep playback running and expose media controls.

## Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose music player and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features change. For privacy questions, contact **abhijitdas021984@gmail.com**.
